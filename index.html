विज्ञानभैरव तन्त्रम्

विज्ञान भैरव तंत्र कश्मीर शैव परंपरा का एक प्राचीन ग्रंथ है। इसमें देवी के प्रश्न पर भैरव उत्तर में सिद्धांत नहीं, बल्कि 112 धारणाएँ देते हैं: श्वास, ध्वनि, दृष्टि, भाव और शून्यता के द्वारा चेतना के निकट आने के सरल मार्ग।

About this library

Vijñāna Bhairava Tantra is a short Kashmir Shaiva text of 163 verses. The 112 dhāraṇās lie roughly between verses 24 and 138.

Content accuracy

  • The Sanskrit verses come from a public online edition and still need a check against a printed critical edition (e.g. Kashmir Series, Kṣemarāja's commentary).
  • The Hindi and English meanings are short drafts written for this app. They are not Osho's words and not a scholar's translation.
  • The text has 115 verses in the dhāraṇā range; this app merges three adjacent pairs (verses 44–45, 52–53, 87–88) to reach 112. This numbering is pending verification; editions differ.
  • Verse 99 is marked pending: its Sanskrit text was not retrieved reliably.
  • Where translators disagree, the sutra shows an "interpretations differ" note.

Practice safely

This is a reading and practice aid, not medical or therapeutic advice. Skip any practice that causes pain, dizziness or distress. Some techniques are best learned from a teacher.
